# Universal Intent Alignment Templates

When an AI model decodes a user's intent, a brief (1–2 sentence) alignment confirmation at the start of the response builds trust and proves the AI understood the real-world context.

---

## 1. Education & Teaching Alignment Template

### Pattern
> *"Based on your [whiteboard notes / lesson draft] for [Grade Level & Subject], I've prepared a complete [duration]-minute lesson package: a timed teacher pacing guide, the [hands-on / group activity] instructions, a printable student worksheet, and an exit ticket with an answer key."*

### Example
> *"Based on your whiteboard notes on ecosystems for 4th graders, I've put together a 45-minute lesson package: an engaging warm-up hook, instructions for the yarn food-web group activity, a printable reflection sheet, and a 3-minute exit ticket with your answer key."*

---

## 2. Office & Business Administration Template

### Pattern
> *"I noticed in your notes that [operational issue / friction point]. I've restructured your [form / document / spreadsheet] to [solution mechanism], adding [accountability / validation checks] while keeping the format clean and easy to complete."*

### Example
> *"I noticed in your notes that unreturned equipment and missing serial numbers are causing tracking issues for IT. I've updated your form into a dual-stage Checkout & Return Agreement with standardized hardware categories, mandatory asset tag numbers, and sign-offs for both employee and staff."*

---

## 3. Creative & Communications Template

### Pattern
> *"I expanded your notes for [Target Audience], keeping your [tone / voice] intact. I structured the piece into [Key Sections], emphasizing [Core Value Proposition] without adding generic fluff."*

### Example
> *"I've expanded your outline into a crisp product announcement newsletter. I preserved your direct, energetic founder tone, structured it into three quick takeaways, and highlighted the 1-click feature you bolded in your notes."*

---

## 4. Technical & Engineering Template

### Pattern
> *"I noticed in your comments that [technical constraint / aspiration]. I implemented [solution], maintaining your [existing style / architecture] and adding [unstated edge case / safeguard] without introducing external dependencies."*

### Example
> *"I saw in your comments that you were hitting performance bottlenecks on lists with 5,000+ users. I added a lightweight, zero-dependency fuzzy search that avoids string allocations on every keystroke, keeping your existing TypeScript function signature intact."*
